Back to first principles.

Arché, a concept explored by early Greek philosophers such as Heraclitus, Anaximander and Aristotle, refers to the origin, the first principle and the underlying essence from which all things emerge and take form, while re, derived from Latin, signifies return, renewal, rebuilding and reconsideration — together forming re.arche: a philosophy centered around returning to first principles to create calmer, clearer and more intentional digital systems stripped of noise, distraction and excess.
